set splitter

drop a concert or band-practice recording. it splits into songs by finding gaps — silence, applause, or talk with no music — and hands you each one as mp3 or wav. runs entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

drop one or more audio files here, or mp3, wav, m4a, flac, ogg — anything the browser can decode. multiple files are stitched together in filename order and treated as one recording.
large files take a moment to decode; long sets are analyzed at about ten times realtime.
