zine formatter

drop a sheet of eight zine pages laid out two rows by four, in reading order. it cuts them up, flips the top row, and rearranges them so the printed sheet folds into a one-page zine. runs entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

drop one or more images here, or png, jpg, webp — anything the browser can open. letter landscape (11 × 8.5) works well.
→
click a page or use the arrow keys to turn. drag to look around.
